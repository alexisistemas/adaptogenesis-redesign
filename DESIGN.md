## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intersection of rigorous clinical functional nutrition and holistic botanical alchemy. Tailored for discerning wellness seekers, bio-optimizers, and health-conscious patrons, it communicates restorative balance, grounded authority, and organic modern luxury.

The aesthetic fuses Organic Modern Luxury with Editorial Polish, evoking the considered elegance of apothecary-grade formulations. The design rejects both sterile clinical minimalism and untamed rustic craft. Instead, it balances generous negative space, warm linen surfaces, soft tactile card surfaces, and precise typographic hierarchy. Visuals feel natural, breathable, and grounded—instilling deep sensory calm and uncompromised commercial clarity.

## Colors

The palette derives from living herbal botanicals, dried adaptogenic roots, and warm natural earth:

- **Primary (`#4A6B53`)**: An earthy, balanced sage green establishing clinical grounding, natural authority, and vitality across key interactive elements and primary headers.
- **Secondary (`#768A65`)**: A botanical warm olive utilized for secondary actions, supportive backgrounds, organic category chips, and soft status indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#C86D51`)**: A vibrant terracotta-coral designed strictly for conversion accents, shopping call-to-actions, cart badges, and limited-run botanical alerts.
- **Neutral (`#F4EFE6`)**: A grounded, sun-bleached linen neutral replacing sterile cool grays with warm, comforting natural fiber tones.

### Complementary Functional Roles
- **Linen Surface (`#FBF9F5`)**: The core canvas tone, providing soft, eye-resting warmth.
- **Forest Ink (`#1B2A22`)**: The definitive typography color, providing crisp, ink-rich contrast while softening the harshness of pure black.
- **Herbaceous Mint (`#9DB39F`)**: Sourced directly from botanical pigments, leveraged for gentle highlights, tags, and subtle tint backdrops.
- **Chartreuse Vitality (`#ADC743`)**: A sharp botanical spark reserved for ingredient spotlights, organic purity certifications, and micro-accents.
- **Deep Spruce (`#14211A`)**: Anchors rich, dark editorial sections, footers, and inverted luxury packaging callouts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes the literary tradition of classical botanical pharmacopeias with the fluid functionality of contemporary digital wellness commerce:

- **Headlines & Display (EB Garamond)**: Infuses timeless editorial beauty, clinical integrity, and artisanal warmth. Headlines should retain their natural case and unforced proportions, avoiding synthetic bold weights to preserve delicate serif terminals.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers clear legibility, gentle geometric curves, and open apertures. It counterbalances the classical cadence of the serif with modern digital clarity, ensuring dosage schedules, ingredient lists, and checkout flows are effortless to scan.
- **Eyebrows & Micro-Badges (`label-caps`)**: Always presented in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.12em`) to introduce structured editorial pacing above large Garamond statements.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy emphasizes generous, deliberate breathing room reminiscent of high-end wellness quarterlies and luxury packaging:

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (`max-width: 1280px`), adapting to an 8-column layout on tablet devices and a 4-column fluid layout on mobile devices.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Generous section spacing (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) on desktop, collapsing gracefully to `space-2xl` on mobile. White space serves as an active ingredient to induce psychological calm and ease decision fatigue during product discovery.
- **E-Commerce Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile` (<640px): Full-width product media cards, sticky bottom action bar, single-column ingredient stories.
  - `Tablet` (640px–1024px): Two-column product grids, split editorial callouts.
  - `Desktop` (>1024px): Three or four-column commerce shelves, split-screen sticky purchasing panels, and staggered editorial botanical profiles.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through organic tonal layering and ultra-soft, warm-tinted ambient shadows that feel bathed in diffused daylight:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - *Base Canvas*: `#FBF9F5` (Linen Surface).
  - *Layered Card Surface*: Pure `#FFFFFF` or subtle `#F4EFE6` overlays.
  - *Inverted Contrast*: Rich `#1B2A22` (Deep Forest) applied to full-width brand immersion banners and luxury footer zones.
- **Shadow Profiles**:
  - *Card Ambient*: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 42, 34, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(27, 42, 34, 0.03)` — soft, herbaceous, and devoid of harsh grayscale tone.
  - *Hover Elevation*: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 42, 34, 0.08), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(27, 42, 34, 0.04)` — gentle upward lift evoking tactile paper stock.
  - *Floating Cart / Modal*: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(20, 33, 26, 0.14)` — grounded containment for transactional overlays.
- **Tactile Outlines**: When borders are required, avoid pure grays. Utilize `1px solid #E4DDD0` (Muted Linen Border) to maintain organic material authenticity.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined Soft geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Elements favor subtle, tactile curves (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`) rather than aggressive or childlike bubble rounds:

- **Cards & Visual Containers**: Default corner radius of `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`), grounding editorial product photos, diagnostic quizzes, and article cards.
- **Form Controls & Inputs**: `0.375rem` radius, presenting a tailored, clinical-grade precision.
- **Badges & Ingredient Tags**: Capsule/pill shape (`rounded-full`) to differentiate functional metadata from actionable interactive cards.
- **CTA Buttons**: Subtle soft edge (`0.375rem` or `0.5rem`) balancing approachable touch points with high-end apothecary sophistication.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (E-Commerce Purchase)**: Terracotta accent (`#C86D51`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `label-button` typography, subtle lift on hover, active state shifts to `#B35D43`.
- **Secondary Action (Learn More / Botanical Profile)**: Deep Sage (`#4A6B53`) background or 1.5px border with Forest Ink text, background transitioning to `#F4EFE6` on hover.
- **Text Link Button**: Forest Ink (`#1B2A22`) with a botanical underline offset by 4px, transitioning to Primary Sage on hover.

### Badges & Chips
- **Functional Benefit Chips**: Small pill-shaped tags (`rounded-full`), background `#F4EFE6`, text `#4A6B53`, displaying benefits such as "Adrenal Support", "Third-Party Tested", or "Wildcrafted".
- **Certification Badges**: Minimalist circular seals incorporating fine botanical iconography and `#ADC743` micro-accents.

### Cards & Packaging Displays
- **Product Commerce Card**: White background over `#FBF9F5` canvas, enclosed in a `1px` Muted Linen border (`#E4DDD0`) with ambient shadow. Features a centered botanical product cutout against an off-white circular matte, followed by an editorial serif title, extraction potency tag, pricing in Jakarta Sans, and a Terracotta "Quick Add" trigger.
- **Ingredient Spotlight Card**: Pale olive tint (`#F4EFE6`), featuring Garamond botanical name (e.g., *Withania somnifera*), origin details, and clinical extract ratio.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Crisp linen fill (`#FFFFFF`), `1px` border (`#E4DDD0`), focus state highlighted by a precise `1.5px` border in `#4A6B53` with zero generic glow rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom square/round controls in `#4A6B53` with organic sage check marks on `#FFFFFF` surfaces.
- **Quantity Selector**: Segmented horizontal control in `#F4EFE6`, featuring tactile decrement/increment buttons with Forest Ink glyphs.

### Cart & E-Commerce Drawer
- Off-canvas slide-out on `#FBF9F5` surface with tiered delivery progress indicators, botanical free-gift milestones, full-item breakdowns, and a prominent sticky checkout block anchored by the Terracotta primary button.
